15.1 Route Map Command
======================

 -- Command: route-map ROUTE-MAP-NAME (permit|deny) ORDER

     Configure the ORDER'th entry in ROUTE-MAP-NAME with 'Match Policy'
     of either _permit_ or _deny_.
