22.5 The zsh/compctl Module
===========================


   The zsh/compctl module makes available two builtin commands.
compctl, is the old, deprecated way to control completions for ZLE. See
*note Completion Using compctl::.  The other builtin command, compcall
can be used in user-defined completion widgets, see *note Completion
Widgets::.
