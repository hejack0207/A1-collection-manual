2 Getting In and Out of GDB
***************************

This chapter discusses how to start GDB, and how to get out of it.  The
essentials are:
   * type 'gdb' to start GDB.
   * type 'quit' or 'Ctrl-d' to exit.

* Quitting GDB::                How to quit GDB
