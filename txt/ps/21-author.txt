AUTHOR
       ps was originally written by Branko Lankester ?lankeste@fwi.uva.nl?.  Michael K. Johnson ?johnsonm@redhat.com? re-wrote it significantly to use the proc filesystem, changing a
       few things in the process.  Michael Shields ?mjshield@nyx.cs.du.edu? added the pid-list feature.  Charles Blake ?cblake@bbn.com? added multi-level sorting, the dirent-style
       library, the device name-to-number mmaped database, the approximate binary search directly on System.map, and many code and documentation cleanups.  David Mossberger-Tang wrote
       the generic BFD support for psupdate.  Albert Cahalan ?albert@users.sf.net? rewrote ps for full Unix98 and BSD support, along with some ugly hacks for obsolete and foreign syn-
       tax.

       Please send bug reports to ?procps@freelists.org?.  No subscription is required or suggested.
