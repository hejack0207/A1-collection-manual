6.2.9. The agentx-CommitSet, -UndoSet, -CleanupSet PDUs

   These PDUs consist of the AgentX header only.

   The agentx-CommitSet-, -UndoSet-, and -Cleanup-PDUs are used in
   processing an SNMP SetRequest operation.
