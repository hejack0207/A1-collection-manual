# ffmvforce(1) - force orientation test for force-feedback devices

ffmvforce, March 8, 2009

```
ffmvforce <device> [-u <update rate>]
```

<a name="description"></a>

# Description

ffmvforce generates a force in a given direction, indicated by the
position of the mouse pointer in relation to the center of the tool's
window.

**Beware, the stress test may damage your device!**

<a name="options"></a>

# Options


* &lt;_device_&gt;  
  The device to test.
* **-u** &lt;_update rate_&gt;  
  The update rate in Hz (5 by default).
