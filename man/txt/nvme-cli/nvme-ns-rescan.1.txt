NAME
       nvme-ns-rescan - Rescans the nvme namespaces.

SYNOPSIS
       nvme ns-rescan <device>

DESCRIPTION
       Requests NVMe controller rescans the namespaces. The <device> param is mandatory and must be an NVMe character
       device (ex: /dev/nvme0).

OPTIONS
       None

EXAMPLES
       o   Rescans the nvme namespaces.

               # nvme ns-rescan /dev/nvme0

NVME
       Part of the nvme-user suite
