NAME
       nvme-reset - Reset the nvme controller.

SYNOPSIS
       nvme reset <device>

DESCRIPTION
       Requests NVMe controller reset. The <device> param is mandatory and must be an NVMe character device (ex:
       /dev/nvme0).

OPTIONS
       None

EXAMPLES
       o   Resets the controller.

               # nvme reset /dev/nvme0

NVME
       Part of the nvme-user suite
