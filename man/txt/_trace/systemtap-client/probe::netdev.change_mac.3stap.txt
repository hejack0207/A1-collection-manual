NAME
       probe::netdev.change_mac - Called when the netdev_name has the MAC changed

SYNOPSIS
       netdev.change_mac

VALUES
       new_mac
           The new MAC address

       mac_len
           The MAC length

       dev_name
           The device that will have the MAC changed

       old_mac
           The current MAC address
