NAME
       probe::scheduler.wait_task - Waiting on a task to unschedule (become inactive)

SYNOPSIS
       scheduler.wait_task

VALUES
       name
           name of the probe point

       task_pid
           PID of the task the scheduler is waiting on

       task_priority
           priority of the task
