NAME
       probe::netdev.receive - Data received from network device.

SYNOPSIS
       netdev.receive

VALUES
       length
           The length of the receiving buffer.

       dev_name
           The name of the device. e.g: eth0, ath1.

       protocol
           Protocol of received packet.
