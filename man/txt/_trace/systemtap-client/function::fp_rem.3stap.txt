NAME
       function::fp_rem - Floating point division

SYNOPSIS
           fp_rem:long(div1:long,div2:long)

ARGUMENTS
       div1
           the 64 bit floating point dividend

       div2
           64 bit floating point divisor

DESCRIPTION
       result would be the remainder after divisor divides dividend
