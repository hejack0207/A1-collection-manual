NAME
       stapfuncs - systemtap functions

DESCRIPTION
       Functions  in  the standard systemtap tapset are individually documented in the 3stap manual section, with the
       function:: prefix.  Some built-in functions such as printf are documented on the stap(1) man page.

SEE ALSO
       stap(1),
       function::*(3stap),
       tapset::*(3stap)
