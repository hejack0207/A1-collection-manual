   dompmwakeup
       Syntax:

          dompmwakeup domain

       Wakeup a domain from pmsuspended state (either suspended by dompmsuspend or from the guest itself). Injects  a
       wakeup  into the guest that is in pmsuspended state, rather than waiting for the previously requested duration
       (if any) to elapse. This operation doesn't not necessarily fail if the domain is running.
