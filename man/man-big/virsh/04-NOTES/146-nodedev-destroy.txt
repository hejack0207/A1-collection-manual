   nodedev-destroy
       Syntax:

          nodedev-destroy device

       Destroy  (stop) a device on the host. device can be either device name or wwn pair in "wwnn,wwpn" format (only
       works for vHBA currently).  Note that this makes libvirt quit managing a host device, and may even  make  that
       device unusable by the rest of the physical host until a reboot.
