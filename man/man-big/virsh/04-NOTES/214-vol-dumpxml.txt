   vol-dumpxml
       Syntax:

          vol-dumpxml vol-name-or-key-or-path [--pool pool-or-uuid]

       Output the volume information as an XML dump to stdout.

       vol-name-or-key-or-path is the name or key or path of the volume to output the XML.

       --pool pool-or-uuid is the name or UUID of the storage pool the volume is in. If the volume name  is  provided
       instead  of the key or path, then providing the pool is necessary to find the volume to be uploaded into; oth-
       erwise, the first volume found by the key or path will be used.
