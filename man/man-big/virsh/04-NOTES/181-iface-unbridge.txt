   iface-unbridge
       Syntax:

          iface-unbridge bridge [--no-start]

       Tear down a bridge device named bridge, releasing its underlying interface back to normal  usage,  and  moving
       all  IP  address  configuration  from the bridge device to the underlying device.  The underlying interface is
       restarted unless --no-start is present; this flag is present for symmetry, but generally not recommended.

       See also iface-bridge for creating a bridge.
