   vol-delete
       Syntax:

          vol-delete vol-name-or-key-or-path [--pool pool-or-uuid] [--delete-snapshots]

       Delete a given volume.

       vol-name-or-key-or-path is the volume name or key or path of the volume to delete.

       [--pool pool-or-uuid] is the name or UUID of the storage pool the volume is in. If the volume name is provided
       instead  of the key or path, then providing the pool is necessary to find the volume to be deleted; otherwise,
       the first volume found by the key or path will be used.

       The --delete-snapshots flag specifies that any snapshots associated with the storage volume should be  deleted
       as well. Not all storage drivers support this option, presently only rbd.
