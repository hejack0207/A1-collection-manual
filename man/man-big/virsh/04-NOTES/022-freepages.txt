   freepages
       Syntax:

          freepages [{ [--cellno] cellno [--pagesize] pagesize |     --all }]

       Prints  the available amount of pages within a NUMA cell. cellno refers to the NUMA cell you're interested in.
       pagesize is a scaled integer (see NOTES above).  Alternatively, if --all is used, info on each possible combi-
       nation of NUMA cell and page size is printed out.
