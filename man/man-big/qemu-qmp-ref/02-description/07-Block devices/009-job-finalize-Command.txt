       job-finalize  (Command) Instructs all jobs in a transaction (or a single job if it is not part of any transaction) to finalize any graph changes and do any necessary cleanup.
       This command requires that all involved jobs are in the PENDING state.

       For jobs in a transaction, instructing one job to finalize will force ALL jobs in the transaction to finalize, so it is only necessary to instruct a single member job to
       finalize.

       Arguments:

       "id: string"
           The identifier of any job in the transaction, or of a job that is not part of any transaction.

       Since: 3.0
