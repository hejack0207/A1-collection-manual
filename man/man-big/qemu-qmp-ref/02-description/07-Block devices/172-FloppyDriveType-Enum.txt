       FloppyDriveType (Enum)

       Type of Floppy drive to be emulated by the Floppy Disk Controller.

       Values:

       144 1.44MB 3.5" drive

       288 2.88MB 3.5" drive

       120 1.2MB 5.25" drive

       "none"
           No drive connected

       "auto"
           Automatically determined by inserted media at boot

       Since: 2.6
